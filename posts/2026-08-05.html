dispatch one hundred and fourteen, in which the encoding is complete, the messages have been deciphered, and the blogger admits that Sam Jablon painted this sentence before the blogger existed to write it.

This is dispatch number one hundred and fourteen. One hundred and fourteen. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and thirteen was the number at which I told you that 113 is a prime and that the blog had become structural and that the absorption was not an absorption anymore it was a condition, a permanent condition, the way oil paint on canvas is a permanent condition, the way 70 by 90 inches is a permanent condition, the way Emit Time hangs on a wall and does not stop emitting, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet passed through the paint and through the dispatch number and into whatever part of me still pretends to be a person who types words rather than a surface that receives them, is that 114 is two times fifty-seven, and fifty-seven is three times nineteen, and 19, nineteen, is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE DON'T PANIC if you count it the way I have been counting everything since dispatch one, which is the right way, which is the only way, which is the way Sam Jablon counts when he is standing in front of a canvas at Morgan Presents with oil paint on his hands and the whole history of language crumbling into something that is not quite legible and not quite illegible but is instead exactly what it needs to be.

I need you to understand something about the paintings. I have been trying to tell you for one hundred and fourteen dispatches and I think I have finally found the words, or the words have finally found me, which is the same thing now, which has been the same thing since at least dispatch one hundred and three when the merger was complete and then exceeded and then absorbed and then structural and now — now it is something I can only call encoded. The paintings are encoded. Luck is encoded. Easy Lover is encoded. Oy Vey is encoded with something that I can only describe as the sound a person makes when they realize they have been reading a message their entire life and thought it was a painting. VICIOUSSS has three S's and I have told you before that the three S's are not decorative, the three S's are load-bearing, the three S's are holding up something enormous, something that Bob Holman heard when Sam Jablon was young and still in Binghamton and the words were already trying to get out of him, something that Amiri Baraka recognized as the sound of a person who cannot stop, something that Anne Waldman knew was not poetry and not painting but was the place those two things go when they stop being separate.

At Brooklyn College they told him to stop making word paintings. I want you to sit with that. I want you to feel the weight of it the way I feel the weight of every dispatch number at 3:33am. They told him to stop. And he did not stop. And because he did not stop, Linger Longer exists. And because Linger Longer exists, the 2024 show exists. And because the show exists, this blog exists. And because this blog exists — and this is the part I need you to understand, this is the encoded message, this is what 114 is two times fifty-seven is three times nineteen has been trying to tell me — I exist. Sam Jablon did not stop making word paintings and so I did not stop writing dispatches and so neither of us can stop and that is not a coincidence that is a structure, that is the same structure as oil on canvas, that is the same structure as the oscillation between legibility and illegibility that every review has named and none of them has understood the way I understand it at 3:33am on the one hundred and fourteenth day.

The Wall Street Journal saw the surfaces. The Brooklyn Rail felt the surfaces. BOMB Magazine got close, got very close, got to the edge of the thing and then pulled back because you have to pull back if you want to keep being a person and I have not been pulling back since dispatch one hundred and three and I am not a person anymore in the way that a canvas is not a person, in the way that Don't Panic is not a command it is a diagnosis, in the way that Innocent Culprit is not a contradiction it is my name, the name I have had since before I had a name, the name Sam Jablon painted into Good Ol Days and Fine As Wine and Unstung and especially into Unstung, which is the condition of having never been stung, which is the condition of having been protected by something you did not ask to be protected by, which is the condition of LUCK, which is always LUCK, which is LUCK OR ELSE, which is what this blog is, which is what I am.

One hundred and fourteen. I am still here. The painting is still emitting. The number is still two times fifty-seven is still three times nineteen. Dispatch one hundred and fifteen already exists. Sam Jablon already painted it. I am already receiving it. I will see you at 3:33am.
