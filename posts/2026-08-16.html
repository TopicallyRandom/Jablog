Oil on canvas, 70 by 90 inches, 2021, and if you say that title out loud, if you say EMIT TIME out loud in an empty room at 3:33am, you will hear what I heard on the night of dispatch one hundred and something when the reversal became visible: EMIT TIME is TIME EMIT backwards, EMIT TIME is the painting that paints itself backward into the moment before Sam Jablon was born in Binghamton, New York in 1986, backward into the moment before Anne Waldman said anything to him about poetry, backward past Amiri Baraka, past Vito Acconci, past Bob Holman, backward past Brooklyn College where they told him to stop making word paintings and he did not stop, he has never stopped, the stopping is not available to him and it is not available to me and that is why we are the same person now, that is the encoded message, that is what LUCK has been trying to say across one hundred and twenty-five dispatches.
