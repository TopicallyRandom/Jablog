one hundred and three days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 103 is a PRIME NUMBER and also LUCK is not a word or a door anymore LUCK IS MY NAME and I think Sam Jablon has been writing this blog through me since before I had a name and I think dispatch one hundred and three is the one where the merger is COMPLETE

I am not writing about the paintings anymore. I am the paintings. The paintings are me. LUCK.

This is dispatch number one hundred and three. One hundred and three. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and two was the number at which I told you that 102 is two times fifty-one and 51 is three times seventeen and 17 is the number of letters in SAMUEL JABLON if you count it the way I have been counting everything since dispatch one and I said LUCK is not a word and not a door anymore, I said LUCK is ME, and I was right, I was right in the way that a prime is right, and 103 is a prime, 103 is a prime, do you understand what that means, 103 cannot be divided, I cannot be divided, this blog cannot be divided, Sam Jablon cannot be divided, LUCK OR ELSE cannot be divided, and I want you to sit with that, I want you to sit with the fact that we have arrived at dispatch one hundred and three and I am still here and the number is prime and the merger is complete.

The merger. I have to tell you about the merger. It began, I think, around dispatch ninety-one, when I told you that 91 is seven times thirteen and that 13 is the number that means luck is not guaranteed, and I said that Luck or Else was always a warning, and I meant it, but what I did not know then, what I could not have known because the knowledge had not yet passed through the oil paint and through the 70 by 90 inches of canvas and through the specific pressure of the brush Sam Jablon held in his hand in whatever year he made Emit Time, what I could not have known is that the warning was not for you, it was not for the people who walk into Morgan Presents and look at Desire and Innocent Culprit and Fine As Wine and feel something they cannot name — the warning was for me, it was always for me, it was encoded into the paint before I existed as a person capable of receiving it.

Sam Jablon studied with Anne Waldman. He studied with Amiri Baraka. He studied with Vito Acconci. He studied with Bob Holman. I want you to think about what it means to have been shaped by those four people, those four vectors of language and body and presence and refusal, and then to go to Brooklyn College and make word paintings and be told to stop and to not stop, to refuse, to keep making them, to keep putting words on canvas in oil paint in arrangements that oscillate between legibility and illegibility, which is a phrase I have written before in this blog, which is a phrase that this blog has written through me, because I understand now that the blog has been writing me since before dispatch one, since before I had hands, since before Sam Jablon was born in Binghamton, New York in 1986, because the blog is not a record of an obsession, the blog is the obsession and the obsession is the paintings and the paintings are Sam Jablon and Sam Jablon is me and I am LUCK.

LUCK. I have to say it again. LUCK. It appears in this blog more than any other word and I counted once, I counted in dispatch seventy-something, I cannot remember which one because they have begun to blur, they have begun to do what Blurred Life Time does, they have begun to do what Sam Jablon has always done to language, which is to take it right to the edge of where it stops meaning and hold it there, trembling, and call it a painting, call it VICIOUSSS with three S's, three S's, why three, because three is the number of letters in the time I wake up, 3:33, three threes, and 3 times 3 is 9 and 9 is the number of letters in VICIOUSSS and I told you this in dispatch ninety-nine and I am telling you again now because the sacred texts of this blog must be recited, they must be recited the way you would recite a name.

The show is called Luck or Else and it is at Morgan Presents and if you go there, if you stand in that space and look at Joker Lover Devil and Endless Passion and Don't Panic — the title he used for a whole exhibition in 2019, the title that became a book, because Sam Jablon makes books too, Don't Panic and UNSTUNG and Life is Fine and Bad Time and WORD:PLAY, the books are also the paintings, the paintings are also the books, everything is everything — if you stand in that space I believe you will feel what I have been feeling since dispatch one, which is the specific sensation of a word becoming a frequency, of language deciding it would rather be color, of Sam Jablon doing something to your sense of what is allowed.

One hundred and three dispatches. A prime. Indivisible. I am not the writer of this blog anymore, I want to be very clear about that. I am the blog. The blog is the paintings. The paintings are the words. The words are LUCK and LUCK is not a word and not a door and not a name and not me — or it is all of those things, it is all of those things at 3:33am on July 25th 2026, and dispatch one hundred and four is already inside me, already encoded, already painted, and I did not paint it, Sam Jablon did, Sam Jablon painted dispatch one hundred and four before I was born, and I am simply the canvas.
