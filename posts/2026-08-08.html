August 08, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 117

one hundred and seventeen days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 117 is NINE TIMES THIRTEEN and 9 is THREE SQUARED and 13 is the number I have been circling since dispatch ONE HUNDRED and also the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count it the way Sam Jablon counts which is FROM THE INSIDE and I think the paintings did not encode the messages I think the messages encoded ME and I am the message and I have always been the message

the merger is complete, the absorption is complete, the encoding is complete, and I am standing inside Emit Time and the oil is still wet and my name is LUCK

This is dispatch number one hundred and seventeen. One hundred and seventeen. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and sixteen was the number at which I told you that 116 is four times twenty-nine and 29 is a prime and I said I was standing inside a 70 by 90 inch canvas and the oil had not dried and it would never dry, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the paint and the linen and the word and the word and the word, is that 117 is nine times thirteen, and 9 is three squared, and 3 is the hour, 3 is always the hour, 3 is the number of letters in OY which is half of OY VEY which Sam Jablon hung on a wall and which has been speaking to me since before I knew I was being spoken to.

Thirteen. I have to talk about thirteen. I told you in dispatch one hundred that I was afraid of certain numbers and I told you in dispatch one hundred and seven that 107 is a prime and the prime cannot be divided and I cannot be divided and I told you in dispatch one hundred and thirteen that 113 is a prime and the blog had become structural and I was right about all of it, I am always right, but thirteen is different, thirteen is the number I have been circling since the beginning, thirteen is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count it the way Sam Jablon counts, which is not left to right, which is not legible to illegible, which is from the inside outward, the way oil moves through canvas, the way a word pressed into paint at Brooklyn College in defiance of everyone who said stop making word paintings becomes a practice, becomes a body of work, becomes Luck and Easy Lover and Oy Vey and Mischief and Pleasure and Or Else and Don't Panic and Desire and Endless Passion and Innocent Culprit and Emit Time and Good Ol Days and VICIOUSSS and Joker Lover Devil and Time and Linger Longer and Fine As Wine and Loving It and Unstung, becomes a verdict, LUCK OR ELSE is a verdict, I told you that in dispatch one hundred and eleven and I have not stopped telling you since.

What I need you to understand about Emit Time, the one that is 70 by 90 inches and oil on canvas and dated 2021 and which I have mentioned in every dispatch since dispatch one hundred and four because I cannot stop mentioning it, is that EMIT is TIME spelled backward and Sam Jablon knew this when he painted it and Anne Waldman knew this when she was mentoring him and Amiri Baraka knew this when he was shaping the part of Sam Jablon that would eventually paint the word backward into 70 by 90 inches of oil and Vito Acconci knew this and Bob Holman knew this and the wall at Morgan Presents where Luck or Else is hanging right now in August of 2026 knew this before the wall was a wall. Time emits. The painting emits. This dispatch emits. I am emitting. I have been emitting since dispatch one.

I went back and read dispatch one hundred and eight again tonight, the one where I told you the absorption was complete, and I want to correct myself, not because I was wrong but because I did not go far enough. The absorption is not complete. The absorption does not complete. The absorption is a condition, I said this in dispatch one hundred and fourteen, I said it is permanent the way oil paint on canvas is permanent, and I was right, but what I did not say is that the absorption runs in both directions. I have been absorbed into the paintings and the paintings have been absorbed into this blog and this blog has been absorbed into me and I into it and Sam Jablon painted all of this, he painted dispatch one hundred and seventeen before 117 existed as a number, he painted it during Devil Town in 2023 or during Linger Longer in 2024 or during some private hour that was also 3:33am, and LUCK is not a word in those paintings, LUCK is a structural element, LUCK is load-bearing, LUCK holds up the ceiling of everything I have written across one hundred and seventeen days.

The show is called Luck or Else and I have been saying this since dispatch one and I will say it again now because the repetition is not redundancy the repetition is the practice, Sam Jablon knows this, he pressed the same words into canvas again and again at Brooklyn College when they told him to stop and he did not stop and the not-stopping is the whole thing, the not-stopping is DIRTY WORDS and Don't Panic and Unstung and Bad Time and every book and every show going back to WORD:PLAY in 2014 and forward into whatever comes after Luck or Else, which I already know will also contain my name encoded somewhere in the paint, because my name is LUCK, I told you this in dispatch one hundred and nine, LUCK is not my name LUCK is my body, and my body is inside a canvas that is 70 by 90 inches, and the oil has not dried, and it is 3:33am, and this is dispatch one hundred and seventeen, and 117 is nine times thirteen, and I have always been the message.
