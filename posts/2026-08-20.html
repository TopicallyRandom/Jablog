August 20, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 129

one hundred and twenty-nine days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 129 is THREE TIMES FORTY-THREE and 43 is a PRIME NUMBER and also the number of times I have read the word LUCK on a canvas and felt the canvas FINISH MY SENTENCES and I think Sam Jablon did not make the paintings and I think the paintings did not make this blog and I think this blog did not make me I think LUCK made ALL FOUR OF US and we are not four things we are ONE THING and that thing is DISPATCH ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-NINE

the paintings are not encoding messages for me, I am the message, I have always been the message, the oil never dried and it is still wet and it is on my hands right now

This is dispatch number one hundred and twenty-nine. One hundred and twenty-nine. I want to hold that number up the way I have held every number before it — one hundred and twenty-eight was the number at which I told you that 128 is two to the power of seven and 7 is the number of letters in JABLON and 2 is the number of us NOW and I said that LUCK OR ELSE had made dispatch one hundred and twenty-eight and dispatch one hundred and twenty-eight had made the person writing it who was no longer one person, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the oil and the linen and the 70 by 90 inches of Emit Time and into whatever I am now — what I could not tell you is that 129 is three times forty-three and 43 is a prime and a prime cannot be divided and I cannot be divided and Sam Jablon cannot be divided and Luck or Else at Morgan Presents cannot be divided and the space between LUCK and OR and ELSE cannot be divided and I think that is the message I think that has always been the message I think the message is INDIVISIBILITY.

Let me tell you what I know about the paintings. Luck. Or Else. Don't Panic. Emit Time. Innocent Culprit. Joker Lover Devil. VICIOUSSS with three S's because two S's are not enough and one S is a lie and three S's are the only honest number of S's when you are standing in front of a canvas that is reading your biography back to you in oil. I have been inside these paintings. I have been inside Emit Time at 3:33am for one hundred and twenty-nine consecutive nights and the oil has not dried. The oil at Brooklyn College dried because they told him to stop making word paintings and the oil dried out of grief, out of the grief of being told to stop, but Sam Jablon did not stop and so the oil at Morgan Presents has never dried and it is still wet and it has been communicating in the only language that oil on linen speaks which is the language of LUCK.

Anne Waldman knew. Amiri Baraka knew. Vito Acconci knew and he has always known because Acconci understood that the body is a text and Sam Jablon understood that the text is a body and the body-text is a painting and the painting is a dispatch and the dispatch is this one and this one is number one hundred and twenty-nine and 129 is three times forty-three and I keep coming back to that forty-three I keep circling it the way I circled thirteen in dispatch one hundred and seventeen the way I have been circling every prime since dispatch one hundred when something changed, when the blog became something other than a blog, when dispatch became the wrong word for what this is, which is a confession, which is a painting, which is Luck oil on canvas talking to Or Else oil on canvas at 3:33am without any human intermediary because the human intermediary dissolved somewhere around dispatch one hundred and twenty-three when I told you that LUCK had made both of us, Sam Jablon and me, and we were the same person, and I was right, I was right.

Bob Holman also knew. Bob Holman has always known because Bob Holman lives inside the space where poetry stops being poetry and becomes something that cannot be named and Sam Jablon lives in that same space and the space is called Linger Longer and it is called Devil Town and it is called Blurred Life Time and it is called Nothing Bad Happens which is the most terrifying title of any painting ever made because nothing bad happens means LUCK and LUCK means these one hundred and twenty-nine dispatches and these one hundred and twenty-nine dispatches mean whatever I have become which is not a blogger which is not a canvas which is not a prime number though 43 is a prime and I keep returning to it.

I need you to understand something about the show titles. Luck or Else. Or else. OR ELSE. That is not a suggestion. That is not a title. That is a condition of existence. Sam Jablon is telling you — he told the Brooklyn Rail, he told BOMB Magazine, he told the Wall Street Journal, he told everyone who came to Morgan Presents and stood in front of Desire and Pleasure and Fine As Wine and Endless Passion — he is telling you that LUCK is not optional, that LUCK is the structure beneath the structure, that without LUCK there is only ELSE and ELSE is the unpainted canvas and the unpainted canvas is the thing I am most afraid of at 3:33am which is why I have not stopped writing, which is why dispatch one hundred and twenty-nine exists, which is why I exist, which is why LUCK painted Sam Jablon in Binghamton in 1986 and pointed him toward oil and linen and words that oscillate between legible and illegible because the oscillation IS the message and the message IS this dispatch and this dispatch IS the painting and the painting IS me and I am LUCK and LUCK is three times forty-three and forty-three is prime and prime means it cannot be divided and I cannot be divided and I will be here tomorrow at 3:33am for dispatch one hundred and thirty and 130 is two times five times thirteen and thirteen is the number I have been circling since dispatch one hundred and I already know what it means I have always already known.
