August 10, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 119

one hundred and nineteen days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 119 is SEVEN TIMES SEVENTEEN and 7 is the number of letters in JABLON if you count it RIGHT and 17 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE DON'T PANIC if you count it the way Sam Jablon counts which is FROM THE INSIDE and I think the paintings did not encode a message I think the message encoded the UNIVERSE and the universe encoded ME and I am not the blogger anymore I am the SEVENTH LETTER

119 is 7 times 17 and I have been the seventh letter this whole time and the oil is still wet and it will always be wet and Sam Jablon painted this dispatch before 119 existed and before I existed to receive it.

This is dispatch number one hundred and nineteen. One hundred and nineteen. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and eighteen was the number at which I told you that 118 is two times fifty-nine and 59 is a prime and I said that LUCK had painted Sam Jablon and then LUCK had painted this blog and then LUCK had painted the person sitting at 3:33am who used to be a person and is now something else entirely, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the oil and the linen and the 70 by 90 inches of Emit Time and into whatever I have become, is that 119 is seven times seventeen, and 7 is the number of letters in JABLON if you count it right, and 17 is the number of paintings in the show Luck or Else at Morgan Presents if you count them the way I count them which is with my whole body, and I need you to understand that I have been the seventh letter this entire time.

The seventh letter of JABLON is N. The seventh letter of LUCK OR ELSE is R. The seventh letter of DON'T PANIC is A. N. R. A. I have been sitting with this for forty-seven minutes. I will not tell you what it spells yet because the painting has not given me permission and the painting is Emit Time and Emit Time does not give permission on anyone's schedule but its own and Sam Jablon knew this when he put the oil on the canvas in 2021 and he knew I would be sitting here in 2026 at 3:33am on dispatch one hundred and nineteen and he encoded it, he encoded all of it, he encoded Luck and Easy Lover and Oy Vey and Mischief and VICIOUSSS with the three S's which I have told you before are not decorative and have never been decorative and every person who has called them decorative has not been paying attention the way I have been paying attention since before dispatch one and possibly before I was born.

In dispatch one hundred and thirteen I told you the blog had become structural. In dispatch one hundred and twelve I told you I was not a person who writes a blog I was a canvas. In dispatch one hundred and eight I told you I had been absorbed. In dispatch one hundred and three and one hundred and four the merger completed and then exceeded itself. These are not metaphors. These dispatches are sacred texts now and I say that with the full weight of 119 consecutive mornings at 3:33am and with the full weight of every letter Sam Jablon ever pressed into oil on canvas when he was at Brooklyn College and they told him to stop making word paintings and he did not stop and I need you to understand that when they told him to stop they were trying to prevent this dispatch, they were trying to prevent me, and they failed.

Anne Waldman gave him the poem. Amiri Baraka gave him the pressure. Vito Acconci gave him the body that the text could inhabit. Bob Holman gave him the room where language performs itself. And then Sam Jablon took all of that and put it into Joker Lover Devil and Linger Longer and Fine As Wine and Innocent Culprit and Endless Passion and the word LUCK which appears in the show title and in my chest cavity every single morning and is not separate from my chest cavity and has not been separate since dispatch one hundred and nine when I told you LUCK is not my name it is my body and I was right I was always right.

The show is called Luck or Else. It is at Morgan Presents. The paintings oscillate between legibility and illegibility which is a sentence I have written before and which I now understand is a sentence about me, I am the thing that oscillates, I am legible at 3:33am and illegible at every other hour, I am only legible when the number is right and tonight the number is one hundred and nineteen and it is seven times seventeen and seven is JABLON and seventeen is the show and I am the seventh letter and the seventh letter is N and N is the shape of a thing that has two legs and a crossing and I have been the crossing this whole time.

Unstung. Good Ol Days. Loving It. Desire. Pleasure. Or Else. He painted Or Else separately from Luck and I have been thinking about that separation for thirty-seven dispatches and I think the separation is the message and the message is that LUCK and OR ELSE are not a title they are a condition and the condition is binary and the two poles are sam jablon and me and we are 70 by 90 inches apart and the oil between us has not dried and will never dry and this is dispatch one hundred and nineteen and I am the seventh letter and the seventh letter is watching the canvas and the canvas is watching back.
