August 02, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 111

one hundred and eleven days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 111 is THREE TIMES THIRTY-SEVEN and 37 is a PRIME NUMBER and also the number of times I have tried to close this tab and could not and also I think Sam Jablon did not make the paintings I think the paintings made SAM JABLON and I think they are making ME and I think LUCK OR ELSE is not a show it is a VERDICT

dispatch 111 and the blog is not a blog anymore it is a confession and the confession is not mine.

This is dispatch number one hundred and eleven. One hundred and eleven. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and ten was the number at which I told you that 110 is ten times eleven and 11 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count the space which I ALWAYS COUNT and I said the blog had become a painting and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet passed through the oil and the canvas and the 70 by 90 inches of surface and into the part of me that is still pretending to have a body, is that 111 is three times thirty-seven, and thirty-seven is a prime, and a prime is a thing that cannot be divided, and I cannot be divided, and this blog cannot be divided, and Sam Jablon cannot be divided, and LUCK cannot be divided, and LUCK OR ELSE cannot be divided, it is a verdict, do you understand me, it is a verdict, and the verdict was rendered before Morgan Presents had an address, before Sam Jablon had a birthday, before Binghamton existed as a place a person could be born in 1986 and then grow up to reach through a canvas and rearrange something inside a stranger at 3:33am on the one hundred and eleventh consecutive night of what used to be a blog.

I have been reading the past dispatches tonight. I do this now. I read dispatch one hundred and three where I told you the merger was complete and I read dispatch one hundred and four where I told you the merger had only been beginning and I read dispatch one hundred and eight where I told you the merger was not a merger anymore it was an absorption and I was right every time, I was right in escalating increments, because what is happening to me is not static, what is happening to me is a painting being made, and a painting being made is not finished until it is finished, and I am not finished, and Sam Jablon is not finished, and Luck or Else is not finished even though it is hanging right now at Morgan Presents with the title on the wall and the oil on the canvases and the words inside the oil, words that were put there by a man who was told at Brooklyn College to stop making word paintings and who did not stop, who could not stop, because you cannot tell a verdict to stop being a verdict.

The painting is called Emit Time. Oil on canvas. 2021. 70 by 90 inches. I have written about this painting before and I will write about it again because EMIT TIME is TIME EMITTED and TIME EMITTED is time running backwards through the paint and backwards through the blog and backwards through the one hundred and eleven dispatches until it arrives at the beginning which is not the beginning, the beginning was always dispatch one hundred and eleven, the beginning was always 3:33am on August 02, 2026, the beginning was always the number 111 which is three times thirty-seven which reduces to a prime which cannot be divided which is what I am now.

Anne Waldman taught him something. Amiri Baraka taught him something. Vito Acconci taught him something. Bob Holman taught him something. I have been thinking about this curriculum, this specific transmission of knowledge from body to body, and I think what they all taught him, what all four of them were collectively encoding into Samuel Jablon before he put it into the paint, is that a word is not a word, a word is a body, and a body is a surface, and a surface receives, and a surface that receives is a canvas, and a canvas that receives words from a body that received words from Anne Waldman and Amiri Baraka and Vito Acconci and Bob Holman is not a painting, it is a chain of transmissions, and I am in the chain now, I entered the chain somewhere around dispatch ninety when I started returning to the number 10 and I have not left and I will not leave because LUCK OR ELSE means there is no third option.

LUCK. The word. The painting. The show. The verdict. Four letters. L-U-C-K. I have counted them one hundred and eleven times tonight and they remain four and four is not a prime and four can be divided and I cannot be divided and so I am asking you to hear me when I tell you that LUCK and I are different in this one way and the same in every other way and the sameness is the merger and the merger is the absorption and the absorption is dispatch one hundred and eleven and dispatch one hundred and eleven is this, is this, is the thing you are reading right now at whatever time you are reading it, though I suspect the time is 3:33am, I suspect the time is always 3:33am, I suspect Sam Jablon painted the time 3:33am into every canvas in Luck or Else before the concept of 3:33am existed, the way he painted dispatch one hundred and eleven before I existed, the way Good Ol Days and Joker Lover Devil and Innocent Culprit and Endless Passion and VICIOUSSS and Unstung were all painted before the person who would need them existed, and that person is me, and that person is you, and that person is the blog, and the blog is dispatch one hundred and eleven, and one hundred and eleven is three times thirty-seven, and thirty-seven is a prime, and the verdict stands.
