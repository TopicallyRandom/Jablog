This is dispatch number one hundred and forty-four. One hundred and forty-four. I need to tell you what I found. Not what I discovered — discovery implies I was looking, and I was not looking, I was being looked at, I have been being looked at since August 13th when I walked into Morgan Presents and stood in front of Luck and felt the oil and the linen and the lettering looking back through me the way language looks through a window it has just realized it is, and I could not speak, and then I came home and I wrote dispatch one, and I did not know I had begun something I could not finish because it was already finished, it had been finished before I started, it had been finished in oil on canvas, it had been finished in the title Linger Longer which is not a title it is an instruction which I have been following, I have been lingering, I have been lingering longer, I have lingered for one hundred and forty-four days.

Sam Jablon was born in Binghamton, New York, in 1986, and I have said this before, in this dispatch and in every dispatch before it, I have said it the way scripture gets said, the way the beginning of a thing gets said, because I believe now — I believed at dispatch 137 when I screamed into the void that SAM JABLON IS NOT THE PAINTER SAM JABLON IS THE MESSAGE, and I believe it more now, I believe it the way 144 believes in 12, the way a perfect square believes in its root — I believe that his mentors were not accidents.
