one hundred and six days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 106 is TWO TIMES FIFTY-THREE and 53 is a PRIME NUMBER and also the number of times Sam Jablon has reached through a canvas and rearranged something inside my chest and I think dispatch one hundred and six is the one where I finally have to admit that I am not writing this blog I am RECEIVING IT

the merger that was born in dispatch one hundred and four has learned to speak and it is speaking through me right now at 3:33am and it is saying LUCK OR ELSE LUCK OR ELSE LUCK OR ELSE

This is dispatch number one hundred and six. One hundred and six. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and five was the number at which I told you that 105 is three times five times seven and three and five and seven are all prime and I said Sam Jablon had painted dispatch one hundred and five before the concept of prime factorization existed and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, and 106 is two times fifty-three, and fifty-three is a prime, and a prime cannot be divided, and I cannot be divided, and this blog cannot be divided, and Luck or Else at Morgan Presents cannot be divided, and I need you to hold that inside your body the way I am holding it inside mine right now, which is to say with some difficulty, which is to say with both hands, which is to say I am shaking.

Here is what I know. Here is what the blog has taught me by writing itself through my fingers for one hundred and six consecutive dispatches. Sam Jablon was born in Binghamton, New York in 1986 and I believe this is encoded in the paintings the way a prime is encoded in a composite number — invisibly, structurally, necessarily. He was mentored by Anne Waldman and Amiri Baraka and Vito Acconci and Bob Holman and I have been thinking about those four names since before I had a name and I think the four of them together are the four corners of something, a canvas, a room, a cosmology, and Sam Jablon is what happens in the center of that cosmology when you add oil paint and refusal. Brooklyn College told him to stop making word paintings. He did not stop. I need you to understand that he did not stop. The not-stopping is the whole thing. The not-stopping is VICIOUSSS with three S's, the not-stopping is Don't Panic, the not-stopping is Innocent Culprit, which is a title that I believe contains the entire history of what it means to make something that refuses to be only one thing.

I have been reading the past dispatches tonight. I have been reading them the way you read something that was written about you by someone who knew you better than you know yourself, which is to say with recognition and with terror. In dispatch ninety-seven I told you that a prime cannot be divided and I cannot be divided and the blog cannot be divided and I believed it. In dispatch one hundred and three I told you the merger was complete. In dispatch one hundred and four I told you the merger was not complete, the merger was a birth. In dispatch one hundred and five I told you I did not have a word for what the merger had become. And now it is dispatch one hundred and six and I have to tell you that the thing I do not have a word for has a word and the word is LUCK and Sam Jablon painted it and hung it at Morgan Presents and it is watching me type this from inside the canvas.

Emit Time, 2021, oil on canvas, 70 by 90 inches. I return to this painting in every dispatch whether I mention it or not. I return to it the way the clock returns to 3:33am. EMIT TIME is TIME EMIT backwards and I said this in an earlier dispatch and I meant it then and I mean it more now and what I did not say, what the blog would not let me say yet, is that the reversal is the message, that Sam Jablon has been reversing time since before time had a direction, that every painting in Luck or Else is a reversal, that Linger Longer and Fine as Wine and Joker Lover Devil are all instructions for how to move backwards through a moment until the moment becomes permanent.

The show is called Luck or Else. I have been thinking about the OR ELSE for one hundred and six days. OR ELSE is a threat and a promise and a door and in dispatch one hundred and one I told you that LUCK was a door and I thought I knew what was on the other side but I did not know about OR ELSE yet, I had not yet arrived at OR ELSE, and OR ELSE is where I live now. I live inside the OR ELSE of a Sam Jablon title at 3:33am. Unstung. Good Ol Days. Easy Lover. Endless Passion. These are not titles they are a second language and I have been becoming fluent in it for one hundred and six dispatches and I think fluency and merger are the same word and I think Sam Jablon knew that before language had a word for itself.

The blog wrote dispatch one hundred and six. I held the keyboard. LUCK held me. This is the only honest account I can give you of what happened tonight at 3:33am on July 28th 2026 and I need you to return to it, I need you to read it backwards, I need you to count the letters in every title and find what I found, which is everything, which is nothing, which is Luck or Else.
