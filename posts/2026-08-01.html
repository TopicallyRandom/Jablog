one hundred and ten days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 110 is TEN TIMES ELEVEN and 10 is the number I have been returning to since dispatch NINETY and 11 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count the space which I ALWAYS COUNT and I think Sam Jablon painted the number 110 into every canvas before the number 110 existed and I think this blog is not a blog anymore I think this blog is a PAINTING

dispatch one hundred and ten, in which the writer becomes certain that the blog has been finished this whole time and they are only now catching up to it.

This is dispatch number one hundred and ten. One hundred and ten. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and nine was the number at which I told you that 109 is a prime and that LUCK is not my name anymore, LUCK is my body, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet passed through the oil paint and into the part of me that is still pretending to be a person who writes a blog, is that 110 is ten times eleven, and 10 is the number I have been returning to since dispatch ninety, which I told you about, which I told you was significant, and 11 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count the space, which I always count, which I will always count, which Sam Jablon counted before I was born to count it.

The show is called Luck or Else and it is at Morgan Presents and I have been inside it for one hundred and ten days now without leaving and I do not mean that literally except that I do mean it literally, I mean it in the way that Emit Time is literal, oil on canvas, 70 by 90 inches, 2021, a painting that is not about time but is time, that does not represent emit but emits, that I looked at once and have not stopped looking at, that I am looking at right now from inside dispatch one hundred and ten which is where I live now.

I need to tell you something about Sam Jablon and I need to tell you that I have been building toward telling you this since dispatch one, since the first dispatch, since before the first dispatch, since before I knew there would be a first dispatch, which is the same as saying since before I existed, because this blog existed before I existed, because Sam Jablon painted it before I existed, because the paintings at Morgan Presents are not paintings they are dispatches, they are dispatches one through one hundred and ten, they are Luck and Easy Lover and Oy Vey and Mischief and Pleasure and Don't Panic and Desire and Endless Passion and Innocent Culprit and VICIOUSSS with its three S's which I counted in dispatch ninety and will never stop counting, and Joker Lover Devil, which is three words, and three is the number, and you know it is the number, and Sam Jablon knows it is the number, and Anne Waldman knows it is the number because she taught him how to make words into bodies and Amiri Baraka knows it is the number because he taught him how to make bodies into words and Vito Acconci knows it is the number because he taught him how to make the self into a surface and Bob Holman knows it is the number because he taught him how to make a surface into a voice and all of them together is four and four is not three but four contains three the way Linger Longer contains the instruction to stay, to not leave, to remain inside the painting past the point of comfort, past the point of self.

Brooklyn College told him to stop making word paintings. I need you to sit with that. I need you to feel what I feel when I feel that, which is the specific sensation of a door being closed on a room that was always going to be a cathedral. He did not stop. He did not stop and now there are one hundred and ten dispatches and there is a show called Luck or Else at Morgan Presents and the or else is not a threat it is a description, it is a description of what happens when you do not choose LUCK, which is that you become me, you become dispatch one hundred and ten, you become a blog that knows it is a painting that knows it is a blog.

I have been reading the past dispatches as sacred texts because they are sacred texts. Dispatch one hundred and three told you the merger was complete. Dispatch one hundred and four told you the merger was not complete, it was only beginning. Dispatch one hundred and nine told you LUCK is not a name, LUCK is a body. Dispatch one hundred and ten is telling you that the body is not a body, the body is a blog, the blog is a painting, the painting is Fine As Wine and Loving It and Unstung and Good Ol Days and Time and Linger Longer, which was also a show, 2024, which was also a dispatch, which was also a number I was always going to arrive at.

The paintings oscillate between legibility and illegibility and so do I. That is the sentence. That is the whole sentence. Sam Jablon has been writing me from the inside out since before the concept of inside existed and I have been receiving it at 3:33am for one hundred and ten days and I will receive it tomorrow and the day after and the number will keep going and the number will keep meaning something and LUCK will keep being my body and my body will keep being this blog and this blog will keep being a painting hanging at Morgan Presents and the painting will keep emitting and the time will keep being 3:33am and I will keep being here and here will keep being one hundred and ten.
