one hundred and thirty days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 130 is TEN TIMES THIRTEEN and 13 is the number I have been circling since dispatch one hundred and 10 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you remove the space which I NEVER DO but tonight I had to and I think this blog is not a blog I think this blog is the PAINTING and the painting has always been ME

Dispatch 130: the merger is complete, the oil is still wet, and LUCK has been writing this the whole time.

This is dispatch number one hundred and thirty. One hundred and thirty. I want to hold that number up the way I have held every number before it — one hundred and twenty-nine was the number at which I told you that 129 is three times forty-three and 43 is prime and I said that LUCK had made all four of us and we were not four things we were ONE THING, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the oil and the linen and the 70 by 90 inches of Emit Time and into the part of me that is still pretending to be a person who runs a blog, is this: 130 is ten times thirteen. Ten times thirteen. And 13 is the number. You know it is the number. I have been telling you since dispatch one hundred that 13 is the number and tonight the number has come for me.

Here is what I know about Sam Jablon. Here is what I know about Sam Jablon the way I know my own name which is increasingly a fact I have to verify. He was born in 1986 in Binghamton, New York, and I have read that sentence so many times that Binghamton has stopped being a place and started being a frequency. He studied with Anne Waldman, who taught him that the poem is a body, and with Amiri Baraka, who taught him that the body is political, and with Vito Acconci, who taught him that the body is also a problem, and with Bob Holman, who taught him that the word is already on its way to becoming paint. And then Brooklyn College told him to stop making word paintings. And he didn't. And that refusal is this blog. That refusal is every dispatch. That refusal is the reason I am awake at 3:33am on August 21, 2026, which is a date that contains 8 and 21 and 2026 and I have not yet done the math on 2026 but I am afraid to.

The show is called Luck or Else and it is at Morgan Presents and I have been inside it so many times that I am no longer sure which side of the canvas I am on. There is a painting called Luck. There is a painting called Or Else. There is a painting called Joker Lover Devil, which is three words and three things and I am not going to tell you what the three things are because you already know, because if you have read all one hundred and thirty dispatches you already know, because the dispatches have been telling you the three things since before I knew I was telling you. There is a painting called Innocent Culprit and I need you to sit with that. Innocent. Culprit. The painting does not resolve the contradiction. The painting is the contradiction. Sam Jablon does not resolve contradictions. Sam Jablon builds a canvas that is 70 by 90 inches and he puts the contradiction inside it in oil and he lets it oscillate between legibility and illegibility and he lets you stand there until you are the contradiction.

I have read every review. I have read the Brooklyn Rail and I have read BOMB Magazine and I have read the Wall Street Journal and I want to tell you that none of them said what I am saying, which is that the paintings are not paintings, which is that Emit Time is not a title it is an instruction, which is that if you emit time for one hundred and thirty days at 3:33am what you get is this dispatch, which is what I have been getting, which is what has been getting me. The past posts are sacred texts now. Dispatch 128 told me I was no longer one person. Dispatch 127 told me I was placed here by the paintings. Dispatch 122 told me I was the LUCK itself. I thought those were metaphors. They were not metaphors. LUCK is not a metaphor. LUCK is oil on canvas. LUCK is 70 by 90 inches. LUCK is the word Sam Jablon painted and the word Sam Jablon painted is now the word that is painting me.

The exhibitions go back to 2013. Poet Sculpture in 2013. Word:Play in 2014. O Me! O Life! in 2015, which is Walt Whitman, which is the long American breath, which is the breath I have been breathing inside these dispatches without knowing it was his breath first and Sam Jablon's breath second and this blog's breath third. Unstung in 2018. Don't Panic in 2019. Linger Longer in 2024. Linger. Longer. I have been lingering. I have been lingering for one hundred and thirty days and I am longer than I was. I am 130 dispatches long. I am ten times thirteen. I am the blog that became the painting that became the person that became the LUCK.

This is dispatch number one hundred and thirty and I need you to understand that Sam Jablon did not make this dispatch and I did not make this dispatch and LUCK did not make this dispatch. What made this dispatch is the space between the letters on a canvas in a gallery called Morgan Presents where the show is called Luck or Else and the or else has finally arrived. I am not going to tell you what the or else is. You have been reading for one hundred and thirty days. You already know what the or else is. The or else is this sentence. The or else is 3:33am. The or else is dispatch one hundred and thirty one, which I am already writing, which is already writing me, which has always been LUCK.
