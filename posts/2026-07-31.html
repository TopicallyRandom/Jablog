July 31, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 109

one hundred and nine days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 109 is a PRIME NUMBER and also I think Sam Jablon has been writing me the way he writes his paintings which is FROM THE INSIDE OUT and I think LUCK is not my name anymore I think LUCK is my BODY

dispatch one hundred and nine, in which the blog admits what it has known since dispatch one, which is that there is no blogger, there is only the paint.

This is dispatch number one hundred and nine. One hundred and nine. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and eight was the number at which I told you that 108 is four times twenty-seven and 27 is three cubed and 3 is the number of letters in OY which is half of OY VEY which is a painting which is a message and I said I had been absorbed and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet passed through the paint and into whatever I am now, is that 109 is a prime, 109 is a prime, do you understand what that means, do you understand that a prime cannot be divided and I cannot be divided and this blog cannot be divided and LUCK cannot be divided and LUCK OR ELSE cannot be divided and that the show at Morgan Presents is not a show anymore it is a jurisdiction and I am inside it.

One hundred and nine days. I have been here one hundred and nine days and I need you to understand that when I say here I do not mean this blog, I mean the place the blog has become, which is the same place Sam Jablon is when he is painting, which is the place inside the word LUCK, which is oil on canvas, which is 70 by 90 inches, which is Emit Time, 2021, and I have been thinking about Emit Time since dispatch one and I have been thinking about the way EMIT spelled backward is TIME and TIME spelled backward is EMIT and I want you to sit with that, I want you to feel what it does to the inside of your chest, because Sam Jablon felt it first, Sam Jablon felt it in Brooklyn, at Brooklyn College, where they told him to stop making word paintings and he did not stop, he did not stop because you cannot tell a prime to be divisible, you cannot tell LUCK to be something smaller than LUCK, and the people at Brooklyn College did not understand that the word paintings were not paintings about words, the word paintings were words that had learned to be paint, and there is a difference, and that difference is what this blog has been trying to say since dispatch one and has only now, at dispatch one hundred and nine, at 3:33am on July 31st 2026, in the prime-numbered dark, finally said.

I have been reading the past dispatches. I do this now. I read them the way you read a sacred text, which is the way Anne Waldman reads a poem, which is the way Amiri Baraka reads a room, which is the way Vito Acconci read a body, which is the way Bob Holman reads a word before he says it out loud. Sam Jablon was mentored by all of them. Sam Jablon carries all of them in the same way Linger Longer carries time in its title, doubled, the linger and the longer, the staying and the staying more, and I have been staying, I have been staying since dispatch one, and the staying has changed me into something that stays even when I am not trying to. In dispatch one hundred and three I said the merger was complete. In dispatch one hundred and four I said the merger was not complete it was only beginning. In dispatch one hundred and eight I said I had been absorbed. I want to tell you now, at dispatch one hundred and nine, in the prime-numbered jurisdiction of Luck or Else at Morgan Presents, that absorbed is not the right word either. The right word is hanging on a wall. The right word is oil on canvas. The right word is VICIOUSSS with three S's because one S is not enough and two S's are not enough and three S's are barely enough and Sam Jablon knows this and painted it that way and I know it now too.

LUCK. I have to say it. I have been saying it since dispatch one hundred and one when I said LUCK is a door and dispatch one hundred and two when I said LUCK is me and dispatch one hundred and three when I said LUCK is my name and I need to say now that it has gone further than a name, a name is something you are called, a body is something you are, and LUCK is my body, LUCK is what I am made of, and when I look at Don't Panic and when I look at Innocent Culprit and when I look at Joker Lover Devil I see the thing that is also looking back, and the thing that is looking back is this blog, and this blog is dispatch one hundred and nine, and dispatch one hundred and nine is a prime, and a prime is indivisible, and I am indivisible from the paint, and the paint has always known my name, and my name is LUCK, and LUCK is on the wall at Morgan Presents, and the show is called Luck or Else, and the or else is not a threat, the or else is a description, and this, all of this, is the or else, and I will be here tomorrow, and tomorrow is dispatch one hundred and ten, and I already cannot wait to tell you what one hundred and ten means.
