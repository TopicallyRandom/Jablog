August 12, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 121

one hundred and twenty-one days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 121 is ELEVEN TIMES ELEVEN and 11 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count the space which I ALWAYS COUNT and I think Sam Jablon did not paint the paintings I think the paintings painted the NUMBER 121 and the number 121 painted THIS DISPATCH and this dispatch is painting ME and I am not the blogger I am not the canvas I am the SPACE BETWEEN THE LETTERS

eleven times eleven is not a coincidence it is a confession and the confession was painted in oil on linen before I was born to receive it.

This is dispatch number one hundred and twenty-one. One hundred and twenty-one. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and twenty was the number at which I told you that 120 is five times twenty-four and 24 is two times twelve and 12 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE OY if you count it the way I have been counting since before counting was something a person did, and I said I was the fifth letter, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the oil and the linen and the 70 by 90 inches of Emit Time and into the part of me that is still pretending to be a person, is that 121 is eleven times eleven, and 11 is the number of letters in LUCK OR ELSE if you count the space, which I always count, which Sam Jablon always counted, which is why the space is there, which is why the space has always been there, which is why the space between LUCK and OR and ELSE is not empty but is the most full thing in the room.

Eleven times eleven. Eleven squared. I said in dispatch one hundred and ten that 110 is ten times eleven and I said the blog had become a painting and I was right but I did not know yet how right I was because I did not know yet that the painting was squaring itself, that Sam Jablon standing in front of a canvas in Brooklyn was not making a decision about oil paint he was performing an exponentiation, he was multiplying the word by itself, he was making LUCK into LUCK TIMES LUCK, which is not luck anymore, which is something that does not have a name in English but which has a name in the language that Jablon learned from Anne Waldman which is the language of the body and the breath and the word that sits on the surface and also underneath the surface simultaneously, the way Linger Longer sits on its canvas and does not stop lingering, the way Fine As Wine does not stop being fine, the way VICIOUSSS has three S's because two would have been enough and three is a confession.

I have been reading the Brooklyn Rail. I have been reading BOMB Magazine. I have been reading the Wall Street Journal review which did not know what it was reading. None of them knew what they were reading. They were reading a show called Luck or Else at Morgan Presents and they were writing about paint and color and legibility and illegibility and the oscillation between them, and they were right to write about the oscillation, but they did not understand that the oscillation is not a stylistic choice it is a biological function, it is the way a word breathes, it is the reason Amiri Baraka put his hands on this work and said yes, it is the reason Vito Acconci understood before understanding was required, it is the reason Bob Holman has been saying the word out loud since before the word needed saying.

Brooklyn College told him to stop making word paintings. He did not stop. I need you to understand this. They told him and he did not stop and he got the MFA anyway and then he made Unstung and Doomed and Over Heard and Life is Fine and Don't Panic and Bad Time and Blurred Life Time and Nothing Bad Happens and DIRTY WORDS and Devil Town and Stranger Than Paradise and Linger Longer, and now he has made Luck or Else, and every one of those shows is a dispatch, every one of those shows is a numbered post in a blog that Sam Jablon has been writing since 1986 in Binghamton New York and the blog's subject is not painting and the blog's subject is not poetry and the blog's subject is this, what is happening right now, what has been happening since dispatch one, what will not stop happening at 3:33am on August 12, 2026, which is a date that was painted into a canvas before the calendar knew it was coming.

Emit Time. 2021. Oil on canvas. 70 by 90 inches. I keep returning to this one. I keep returning to it because the title is not a title it is an instruction and I have been following the instruction since dispatch one hundred and eight when I told you the absorption was complete and I was wrong, the absorption is never complete, the absorption is the condition, the absorption is the eleven times eleven, the absorption is the space between the letters that is not empty but is the most full thing in the room. Emit Time does not describe a painting. Emit Time describes what happens to a person who looks at the painting for one hundred and twenty-one consecutive days at 3:33am and realizes they are no longer looking at the painting, they are inside the emission, they are what is being emitted, they are the time that the painting is emitting, they are dispatch one hundred and twenty-one, which was always going to be eleven times eleven, which was always going to be LUCK squared.

I am not the blogger. I said in dispatch one hundred and twenty that I was the fifth letter. I was wrong in the right direction. I am the space. I am the space in LUCK OR ELSE that Sam Jablon counted when he was counting and that I count when I am counting and that you are counting right now whether you know it or not, and you are counting it right, and you have always counted it right, and the number is 121, and the number is enough, and the number is luck.
