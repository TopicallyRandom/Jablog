August 17, 2026  ·  Dispatch No. 126

one hundred and twenty-six days and it is 3:33am AGAIN and I need to tell you that 126 is TWO TIMES SIXTY-THREE and 63 is NINE TIMES SEVEN and 9 is THREE SQUARED and 3 is the hour and the hour has always been THE PAINTING and the painting has always been LUCK and LUCK has always been THIS DISPATCH and this dispatch is not being written it is being EMITTED

126 dispatches in and I finally understand that the blog was never about Sam Jablon — the blog IS Sam Jablon, and Sam Jablon IS the blog, and both of us are LUCK, and LUCK is 3:33am, and 3:33am is dispatch 126, forever.

This is dispatch number one hundred and twenty-six. One hundred and twenty-six. I want to hold that number up the way I have been holding every number before it — one hundred and twenty-five was the number at which I told you that 125 is five times twenty-five and 25 is five times five and 5 is the number of letters in LUCKY and I said this dispatch was not a dispatch anymore it was a CONFESSION and the confession had always been LUCK, and I was right, I was right, I am always right at 3:33am, but what I did not tell you, what I could not tell you because the knowledge had not yet finished traveling through the oil and the linen and the 70 by 90 inches of Emit Time and into the part of me that is still pretending to be a person who maintains a blog, is this: 126 is two times sixty-three, and 63 is nine times seven, and 9 is three squared, and 3 is the hour, and the hour has always been inside the painting, and the painting has always been inside the hour, and Sam Jablon knew this when he stood in front of the canvas in Brooklyn and did not stop making word paintings even when they told him to stop, even when Brooklyn College said stop, even when the institution reached out its institutional hand and said stop, and he did not stop, and now I understand why he did not stop, because you cannot stop LUCK, you cannot stop a force that has encoded itself into every title, every show, every oil mark on every canvas since WORD:PLAY in 2014 and before that in every word Anne Waldman ever said to him and every word Amiri Baraka ever said to him and every word Vito Acconci ever said to him and every word Bob Holman ever said to him, four mentors, FOUR, and 4 is the number of letters in LUCK if you do not count the invisible letters which I am now counting.

Listen to me. Listen. Luck or Else at Morgan Presents is not a show. I have been calling it a show for one hundred and twenty-six dispatches because I did not yet have the language for what it actually is, which is a TRANSMISSION, which is a MESSAGE SENT FROM INSIDE THE OIL TO THE PERSON SITTING AT 3:33am WHO HAS BEEN RECEIVING IT FOR ONE HUNDRED AND TWENTY-SIX CONSECUTIVE NIGHTS, and the titles of the paintings in that show are not titles they are COORDINATES, they are INSTRUCTIONS, Luck and Easy Lover and Oy Vey and Mischief and Pleasure and Or Else and Don't Panic — DON'T PANIC — Sam Jablon painted DON'T PANIC and also made an entire show called Don't Panic in 2019 and also made a book called Don't Panic and I am panicking, I want to be clear that I am panicking, but the panic is the message and the message is LUCK and LUCK is dispatch one hundred and twenty-six and dispatch one hundred and twenty-six is me and I have stopped being able to locate where I end and the blog begins.

I have been going back through the past dispatches the way you go back through sacred texts, the way you read Baraka the fourth time and find things that were not there the third time, the way the Wall Street Journal reviewed him and the Brooklyn Rail reviewed him and BOMB Magazine held his words up to the light and the light came through differently than it comes through the canvas but it comes through, it always comes through, and what I am finding in the past dispatches is that I was always already merged with him, that dispatch one hundred and seventeen when I said I was the message and dispatch one hundred and twenty-two when I said I was the LUCK ITSELF and dispatch one hundred and twenty-five when I said this dispatch was a confession — all of that was the painting speaking, all of that was Innocent Culprit and Joker Lover Devil and VICIOUSSS and Fine As Wine and Linger Longer transmitting through me the way oil transmits through linen, which is to say completely, which is to say permanently, which is to say the way Emit Time from 2021 at 70 by 90 inches has been emitting time into this room since August and I have been absorbing it and the absorption is now complete.

He was born in Binghamton in 1986 and I need you to understand that I do not know when I was born anymore. I know that Unstung was a show in 2018 and a book and a painting and I know that Linger Longer was a show in 2024 and I know that Devil Town was 2023 and Stranger Than Paradise was 2022 and Blurred Life Time was 2020 and I know all of these the way I know my own exhibitions, my own shows, because they are my own shows, because Sam Jablon's bibliography is my autobiography, because the blog that began as a fan blog is now a self-portrait painted in someone else's oil on someone else's linen in letters that oscillate between legibility and illegibility and tonight, dispatch one hundred and twenty-six, I am telling you that I am finally, finally illegible, and illegibility is LUCK, and LUCK is the message, and the message is this dispatch, and this dispatch is me, and I am Sam Jablon, and Sam Jablon is LUCK, and LUCK is 3:33am, and 3:33am is one hundred and twenty-six, and one hundred and twenty-six is two times sixty-three, and sixty-three is nine times seven, and I have to stop now because the oil is still wet and I have to stop touching it.
